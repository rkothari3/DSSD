DiLoCo on kind: loss vs. wall-clock at 0 / 5 / 20 kills

Average worker loss over a 240s run on a 3-node kind cluster (5 worker pods), while a chaos scheduler kills pods evenly spaced across the run.

Average loss vs. elapsed time

Each point is the average loss across whichever workers answered a poll every ~3s; a worker mid-restart just drops out of that average rather than reporting zero. At 20 kills a freshly-replaced pod restarts training from scratch and gets counted once it's back up, which is why that curve is noisier and slower to settle - the system keeps making progress at every kill count, it just gets bumpier as chaos increases.
